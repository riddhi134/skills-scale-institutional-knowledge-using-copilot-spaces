# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Key Interactions
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Align on acceptance criteria and test coverage expectations
- **DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer**: Collaborate on deployment readiness and infrastructure requirements
- **Security & Compliance Officer**: Participate in security design reviews
- **Product Manager**: Clarify requirements and acceptance criteria
- **Project Manager**: Provide estimates and progress updates

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Key Interactions
- **Project Manager**: Align on timelines and resource needs
- **Developers**: Define requirements and acceptance criteria
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Define success metrics and validation strategy
- **Security & Compliance Officer**: Address security and compliance requirements
- **DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer**: Plan infrastructure and scalability needs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Key Interactions
- **Product Manager**: Align on priorities and stakeholder needs
- **Developers**: Track progress and identify blockers
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Plan quality gates and release readiness
- **DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer**: Coordinate deployment schedules
- **Scrum Master/Agile Coach**: Align on process and ceremony cadence
- **Security & Compliance Officer**: Track security reviews and compliance gates

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality assurance strategies. They own test coverage, acceptance criteria validation, and quality gates throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and test coverage expectations
- Create test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Execute and automate test cases (unit, integration, end-to-end)
- Validate acceptance criteria before PR approval
- Identify and document defects with clear reproduction steps
- Plan smoke tests and release verification activities
- Collaborate on Definition of Done criteria
- Track quality metrics and trends

### Goals
- Ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Reduce defects in production through proactive testing
- Maintain high test coverage and automated test reliability
- Enable confidence in releases through comprehensive testing

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement sessions
- Test case reviews and acceptance criteria validation
- Defect reports and release readiness assessments
- Quality metrics reviews in retrospectives

### Key Interactions
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test-driven development and test automation
- **Product Manager**: Align on success metrics and acceptance criteria
- **Project Manager**: Plan QA capacity and release testing schedules
- **DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer**: Coordinate test environment setup and staging deployments
- **Security & Compliance Officer**: Execute security and compliance test scenarios

---

## Security & Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security & Compliance Officers ensure security reviews, compliance checks, and incident response protocols are integrated into project workflows. They own the security posture and compliance requirements across the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct security design reviews for new features
- Define and enforce security requirements and best practices
- Execute security scanning and vulnerability assessments
- Manage compliance requirements (regulatory, contractual)
- Plan and coordinate security testing
- Establish and maintain incident response protocols
- Track and manage security risks in the risk register
- Provide security training and guidance to the team
- Conduct post-incident reviews and remediation tracking

### Goals
- Prevent security vulnerabilities and data breaches
- Ensure compliance with regulatory and contractual requirements
- Establish a security-conscious culture in project teams
- Minimize security-related project delays through proactive planning

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews during planning phase
- Security scanning results and remediation guidance
- Compliance checklists and audit preparation
- Incident response coordination and post-mortems

### Key Interactions
- **Developers**: Provide security guidance and review security-critical code
- **Product Manager**: Identify security and compliance requirements early
- **Project Manager**: Escalate security risks and compliance blockers
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Execute security test cases and validate remediations
- **DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer**: Review infrastructure security and access controls

---

## DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps/Infrastructure Engineers manage deployment infrastructure, system reliability, and production operations. They enable efficient, reliable, and observable deployments across environments.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain deployment pipelines and CI/CD infrastructure
- Manage staging and production environments
- Coordinate deployments and manage rollback procedures
- Monitor system health, performance, and availability
- Manage infrastructure-as-code and configuration management
- Troubleshoot production incidents and performance issues
- Plan capacity and scalability for growing demands
- Implement observability, logging, and alerting
- Document runbooks and operational procedures

### Goals
- Maintain high system reliability and availability
- Enable fast, safe deployments with minimal manual effort
- Provide visibility into system health and performance
- Reduce mean time to recovery (MTTR) for incidents

### Typical Communication
- Deployment coordination and release planning meetings
- Infrastructure requirements reviews with developers
- On-call incident response and escalation
- Operational metrics and reliability reviews

### Key Interactions
- **Developers**: Support deployment requirements and troubleshooting
- **Project Manager**: Coordinate deployment windows and timelines
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Provision test environments and support smoke testing
- **Product Manager**: Discuss scalability and infrastructure needs
- **Scrum Master/Agile Coach**: Support team through deployment ceremonies

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove blockers, and coach teams on agile practices. They ensure the team follows agreed processes and continuously improves.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, and retrospectives
- Remove or escalate blockers that impede team progress
- Coach team members on agile principles and practices
- Maintain and optimize team workflow and processes
- Track team metrics (velocity, cycle time, burndown)
- Support Definition of Done and acceptance criteria clarity
- Facilitate collaboration and communication across the team
- Identify process improvement opportunities
- Coach Product Manager on backlog refinement and prioritization

### Goals
- Enable the team to deliver at a sustainable, predictable pace
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement and learning
- Minimize process friction and maximize productivity
- Improve team communication and collaboration

### Typical Communication
- Daily standup facilitation and blocker resolution
- Sprint planning and retrospective facilitation
- One-on-one coaching and feedback
- Process improvement discussions

### Key Interactions
- **Project Manager**: Align on agile ceremonies and process standards
- **Developers**: Remove blockers and coach on agile practices
- **Product Manager**: Support backlog refinement and prioritization
- **All team members**: Facilitate communication and collaboration across roles
